# Methods

This study maps scientific literature and official documents addressing regulation, legislation, and institutional responsibilities related to cultivated meat in Brazil.

## Eligibility

Include studies, theses, articles, and documents addressing regulation, legislation, institutional jurisdiction, or the Brazilian legal framework; Portuguese, English, or Spanish; and international comparative studies that include Brazil. Exclude purely technical studies without a regulatory component, sources outside Brazil unless comparative, inaccessible paid sources, and news items without substantive analysis.

## Extraction

Record source, source type, issuing body/author, year and status, regulatory stage, indicated competence, defined requirement, gap or ambiguity, evidence page/excerpt, and legal provision.

## Synthesis

Compare safety assessment, authorization, product registration, inspection, labelling, and commercialization. Scientific and legal literature contextualizes interpretation; claims about legal competence and validity must be anchored in verifiable official documents.

## Source categories

`data/processed/official_documents.csv` uses these mutually exclusive categories:

- **Current norm:** in force at the time recorded.
- **Historical norm:** superseded, revoked, consolidated, or otherwise retained for historical context.
- **Preparatory act:** consultation, agenda, draft, technical note, or other preparatory instrument.
- **Legislative proposal:** bill or other proposal not yet enacted.
- **Tertiary source:** commentary or institutional summary that is not itself a primary legal instrument.

The supplied official-document list contains current and historical norms. No preparatory act or legislative proposal appears in that list. The GFI Brazil declaration is a tertiary source referenced in the extraction matrix.
